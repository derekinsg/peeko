Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

This policy explains what Peeko collects, how it is used and how long it is kept.

What we collect

Photos: the photos you take or choose. Location data is removed before upload.

Recent photos (only if you agree): if you turn on "Make stickers from recent photos", the app reads photos from the last month in your library each time you open it and checks them on your phone first: screenshots, photos with people and photos full of text (such as documents or chats) are never uploaded. Up to 10 photos a day that pass are uploaded to pick words and made into stickers for you to open. This is off by default, and you can turn it off in Settings at any time; once off, the app stops reading your library.

Account details: your email address if you sign in with Apple or Google, and your account ID.

Learning data: your scenes, words, review history, native language, the language you are learning and your level in it, and accent and reminder settings.

Purchase records: the subscription product, its expiry date and Apple's transaction number. We never see your payment details.

Device flag: we use Apple's DeviceCheck to record whether this device has used the free trial. It cannot identify you personally.

We do not collect location data, and we do not use advertising or analytics tools.

How we use it

Photos are sent to OpenAI's model to choose words worth learning, with storage turned off for those requests. When you tap part of a photo to look up a word, a small crop of it is sent too.

To read a word and its example sentence aloud in a natural voice, that text (never your photo) is sent to OpenAI's speech model. The audio is kept on our servers so the same sentence is not made twice.

Cutting objects out into stickers happens on your phone. Cut-outs are not uploaded.

Where it is kept, and for how long

Data and photos are stored with Supabase, on servers in Singapore.

  • Scenes you did not save, including ones made automatically that you never saved: deleted with their photo after 24 hours.
  • Trial accounts that never signed in: the account and all its data are deleted 30 days after last use.
  • Saved scenes: kept until you delete the scene or your account.

Your rights

You can delete a scene in the app at any time. Settings → Account → Delete Account deletes your account, and all your data and photos on our servers are deleted right away.

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription, so cancel it in Apple's subscription settings too.

To export your data, email semantigai@gmail.com and we will handle it by hand.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13.
