Peeko Support

Peeko turns the photos you take into vocabulary: words land right on the things in your photo, in the language you are learning, with a natural voice and reviews built from your own pictures.

Contact

Email semantigai@gmail.com. We reply within two working days.

Subscriptions

Peeko Pro raises the limit from 3 photos a day to 50. Review and the word book stay free. To cancel, open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions. To move a subscription to a new phone, open Peeko's paywall and tap Restore Purchases.

Deleting your account

In Peeko, open Settings → Account → Delete Account. Your account and all your photos and words are deleted from our servers right away. Deleting the account does not cancel a subscription; cancel it in Apple's settings too.

A word looks wrong

Words, translations and examples are made by an AI model and can be wrong. Mark the word as known to hide it, or tell us by email with a screenshot.
